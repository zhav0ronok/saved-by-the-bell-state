#### Assumed Background

At the time of starting this blog, I have already completed (and will therefore be writing from the perspective of someone who has taken) an introductory course on Quantum Mechanics (which covered a small amount of QC towards the end) and the standard undergraduate introductory physics sequence (for my school, at least):

- Mechanics & Special Relativity
- Electricity and Magnetism
- Waves and Thermodynamics

As well as the introductory math sequence for physics majors:

- Calculus I, II, and III
- Linear Algebra
- Differential Equations

I will still do my best to keep things self-contained, as it pertains to the topics in this blog.

#### Recommended Reading

If you lack a background in quantum mechanics and would like somewhere to start, I suggest the following textbooks:

- Quantum Mechanics *A Paradigms Approach*; David H. McIntyre
- Introduction to Quantum Mechanics *Second Edition*; David J. Griffiths

I am currently reading:

- Quantum Computation and Quantum Information *10th Anniversary Edution*; Michael A. Nielsen & Isaac L. Chuang
